# Client acquisition playbook — post-clearance only

**Operating model:** Claude assembles and drafts; Jon spends one ~20-minute
block per week sending from his own email and taking calls. Nothing is sent
by an agent on Jon's behalf — every outbound goes out under his hand.

## Weekly cadence (run by the Monday routine once #44 = GRANTED)

1. **Lead list:** routine compiles 10 fresh leads matching the ICP
   (positioning.md) from: WeWorkRemotely + RemoteOK contract listings, YC
   directory (data/devtools/fintech-adjacent, excluding PH-regulated), and
   "fractional/part-time senior engineer" posts. Screens every lead against
   the compliance boundaries **before** it reaches the list.
2. **Drafts:** routine attaches a personalized 3-sentence email per lead
   (template below) to the scoreboard issue.
3. **Jon's 20 minutes:** send the drafts, log replies with a comment
   ("replied: 2, call Thu").
4. **Routine follow-up:** drafts the follow-ups (day 4 and day 10, then stop),
   preps discovery-call notes, tracks pipeline in the scoreboard table.

Stop condition: **first signed retainer → pause all outreach.** Depth over churn.

## Cold email template (first touch)

Subject: `fractional senior backend help — 8h/wk`

> Hi [Name] — saw [specific thing: job post / launch / stack mention]. I'm a
> senior engineer embedded at a central bank (Postgres, TypeScript, CI/CD,
> finishing an MS in Financial Engineering) with one 8–10 h/wk retainer slot
> open, and
> [company]'s [specific problem: data pipeline / query performance / release
> automation] is exactly the shape of work I do best.
>
> Recent public work: github.com/amadogabriel/life-os — production-grade React
> + Supabase with e2e coverage and documented architecture decisions.
>
> Worth a 20-minute call this week? If the fit's wrong, happy to say so fast.

Follow-up (day 4): one line — "Floating this up — still have the slot open
this month. Worth 20 minutes?" Day 10: close the loop and move on.

## Discovery call agenda (30 min, Claude preps a brief per call)

1. Their bottleneck (10) → 2. What done-in-30-days looks like (5) →
3. How I work: async, retainer, docs, one client (5) → 4. Compliance
disclosure sentence (1) → 5. Price and start date, said plainly (5) → 6. Next
step in writing before hanging up (4).

## Retainer SOW skeleton (Claude drafts per client)

Scope: up to N h/wk, async, [areas]. Fee: $[X]/mo, invoiced monthly in
advance, 8% BIR receipts. Comms: async written, 24–48h response, no standing
meetings before 9pm PHT. IP: work-for-hire on payment. Termination: 30 days'
notice; immediate if consultant's employment or confidentiality obligations
require it. Exclusions: entities under Philippine financial supervision;
procurement-related work.

## Pipeline math (why this converges)

Cold outreach at this seniority converts ~2–5% to calls; calls to retainer
~1-in-4. Ten sends/week ≈ one or two calls/month ≈ **first retainer inside
8–12 weeks**, in parallel with vetted-network acceptance (24–72h matching once
in). Whichever lands first fills the slot and stops the machine.
